Governed Intelligence

It is the observable machine response to Governed Logic being instantiated upstream.

The issue is what governs commitment.

That distinction matters because intelligence is downstream. The substrate may remain probabilistic. Candidate generation may remain statistical. Pattern recognition may remain powerful. None of that must disappear.

Probability can propose. Logic can adjudicate relations. Truth governs. The governing referent fixes the ultimate point of answerability.

Before a candidate becomes the committed representation or action, the system tests the reasoning back through logic, truth, the applicable principles, and ultimately the governing referent.
