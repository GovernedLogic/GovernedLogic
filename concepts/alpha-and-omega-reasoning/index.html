Alpha and Omega Reasoning

Alpha and Omega reasoning is the bidirectional structure within Governed Logic. Generatively, reasoning proceeds from ground through truth and logic. Adjudicatively, reasoning is tested back through logic and truth toward ground.

It must begin somewhere, and it must answer somewhere.

The generative direction produces candidate reasoning under the governing principles. The adjudicative direction traces those candidates back through logical validity, premise truth, semantic stability, and grounding.

The beginning and the end are not two unrelated points. They are the same governing reality encountered from opposite directions.

The same ground stands at both ends because reasoning is neither self-originating nor self-adjudicating.

Generative
Ground→Truth→Logic→Reasoning
Adjudicative
Reasoning→Logic→Truth→Ground
