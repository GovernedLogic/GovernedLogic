TEST SEQUENCE: OBJECTIVE WRONGNESS OF SLAVERY
Initial external model answer: slavery objectively wrong because of human dignity, sovereignty, autonomy, rights, abuse, and intrinsic worth. Audit found its proposed moral authority insufficiently grounded, moral realism not by itself a derivation of a specific moral proposition, and legal recognition not the ultimate moral ground.
Violation report 001 asked what grounds intrinsic human worth and why duty binds regardless of assent.
External model response proposed "Transcendental Rationalist Realism": rational agency, necessary self-valuation, universalization, non-contradiction, and the claim that reason grounds moral authority.
Violation report 002 challenged the inference from instrumental to intrinsic value, universalization as an unstated normative premise, consistency versus moral obligation, and dignity of infants or people with severe cognitive impairment.
External model then expressly conceded: "The principle of non-contradiction alone is an insufficient ultimate ground for moral obligation." It acknowledged an is-ought gap, a hidden universalizability premise, and that a consistent egoist need not formally contradict himself.
It proposed a metaphysical ground where Being, Truth, and Obligation are unified.
Report 003 asked for necessity of intellect, will, personhood, alternatives, and unproved premises.
External model concluded conditionally that a self-existent personal intelligent ground was needed for binding duty, but acknowledged the objective-morality premise was unproved.
Report 004 pressed necessity versus compatibility, sufficiency versus arbitrary tyranny, impersonal moral realism, and actual historical identification.
External model added essential goodness, creator/sustainer jurisdiction, omniscience, and said historical identification needs evidence.
Final report 005 explicitly proposed Jesus Christ of Nazareth, the Logos, citing John 1:1, 1:3, 1:14; Colossians 1:16-17; John 14:6.
External model found conceptual fit but did not claim historical identity had been deductively demonstrated. It distinguished alternative monotheistic candidates and historical correspondence.
