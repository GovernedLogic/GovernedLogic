The Core Idea

Truth governs logic.
Logic governs reasoning.
Intelligence is downstream.

Governed Logic provides a clear, structured framework for
reasoning about reality, aligning artificial and human intelligence
with truth, and examining the most fundamental questions about
ultimate grounding, meaning, and moral obligation.

The Problem

Current AI Reasoning
Has Fundamental Defects.

Large language models can produce fluent, convincing, and useful
answers, but they often lack a reliable grounding in truth, logic,
and reality. This leads to systematic errors, false confidence,
and unpredictable behavior.
