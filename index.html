01 / Why the company exists

Modern AI has a reasoning problem.

Modern AI is artificially intelligent. It can generate fluent arguments, synthesize enormous amounts of information, and perform increasingly sophisticated inference.

But capability is not the same thing as correct reasoning. More capability does not repair a flawed reasoning process.

What we are building
02 / The hidden flaw beneath modern AI

The groundless machine.

Current AI does not know what truth is. Its reasoning is not governed by truth.

That is the upstream problem. Intelligence is downstream.

If the reasoning system is groundless, increasing intelligence only increases the capability of a groundless machine.
