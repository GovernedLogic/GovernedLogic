02The problem

The Groundless Machine.

The Hidden Flaw Beneath Modern AI

A system can become more capable without becoming better grounded. It can generate more fluent arguments, synthesize more information, perform longer inference chains, and imitate increasingly sophisticated human discourse while still lacking a stable governing referent for adjudicating the frameworks from which those outputs are produced.

Generatively, reasoning proceeds from ground through truth and logic. Adjudicatively, reasoning is tested back through logic and truth toward ground.

Logic alone cannot determine truth. A valid argument can proceed from false premises. Truth therefore governs the admissibility of the premises and the correspondence of the conclusion to reality. Logic governs the relations of inference.

06The research

The objective is truth.

Hold the framework constant.
Change the governing referent.

Model behavior can provide evidence about the effects, stability, and failure modes of an implementation. It cannot independently prove a metaphysical thesis, a historical claim, or the identity of the ultimate ground.

A system that cannot be wrong cannot be tested. A framework that cannot lose cannot be falsified.
