Navigation behavior

  • Search is deferred until the corpus is large enough to justify it; HTML metadata and clear index pages serve discoverability first.

Visual design specification

Identity

Dark charcoal field, cool metallic blue/silver mark, precise linework, restrained warm accent, linked three-loop motif, “Governed Logic / Reasoning Systems,” and “Follow the Chain.”

Logo and diagrams

  • The smooth three-loop linked form is the preferred direction, subject to exact master-mark approval. Preserve a one-color version for small and accessibility-constrained contexts.
  • Use the mark as identity, not as proof. Do not force every diagram into the logo geometry.
  • Use SVG/HTML diagrams with selectable text, named nodes, and textual equivalents. The primary homepage visual should explain governance order, not simulate a mysterious neural network.

Motion and accessibility

  • No scroll-jacking, mandatory scroll snapping, continuous particle fields, auto-playing audio, or motion required to understand content.
  • Allow one restrained, interruptible “follow the chain” reveal; provide an immediate static state and honor prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings, skip link, labeled controls, alt text, and 200% zoom/reflow.
  • Home cards collapse to one column on narrow screens. Reading pages remain approximately 65–75 characters per line.
  • Print styles preserve articles, papers, and ledgers without dark backgrounds or clipped tables.
