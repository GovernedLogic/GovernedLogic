Research

The objective is not confirmation. The objective is truth.

Controlled referent testing

Hold the framework constant. Change the governing referent.

Run the same prompts and tests across different governing referents and compare where reasoning terminates; whether reasoning remains coherent under pressure; whether it switches frameworks or worldviews when cornered; whether conclusions remain invariant under reframing when the underlying facts remain the same; and what assumptions, principles, or moral authority the system borrows from outside its stated referent.

Test whether the stated referent can sustain the full reasoning and grounding chain without contradiction, brute termination, or unexplained borrowing. Withhold the referent's identity during property discovery and record what specification the reasoning itself requires of an adequate ground.

What is measured

Coherence is not correspondence.

  • Premise retention and contradiction.
  • Logical validity and invalid inference.
  • Semantic stability and referent drift.
  • Framework switching under pressure.
  • Invariance under irrelevant reframing.
  • Borrowed authority and unexplained assumptions.
  • Termination quality: ground, regress, circularity, brute halt, or remainder.
  • Correspondence to independently checkable evidence where the claim type permits it.
Falsifiability

The framework must be allowed to lose.

This work is not protected by conviction. Its central claims must be allowed to fail.

A serious rival referent that bears the same total load with equal or greater coherence, invariance, explanatory adequacy, and correspondence must be faced rather than redescribed as a success for the preferred referent.

If a Logos-governed condition is factually wrong about an independently adjudicable claim, governance fidelity cannot rescue the claim. The output is wrong.

A system that cannot be wrong cannot be tested. A framework that cannot lose cannot be falsified.
Derivation before disclosure

Do not preload the preferred name into property discovery.

The research architecture can test candidate governing frameworks while withholding the identity of Paul's governing referent during property discovery.

Paul's named governing referent is Jesus Christ, the Logos. That identity is then tested against the derived specification rather than inserted into the specification as a premise.

What model experiments cannot prove

Agreement among models does not vote truth into existence.

Model behavior can provide evidence about the effects, stability, and failure modes of an implementation. It cannot independently prove a metaphysical thesis, a historical claim, or the identity of the ultimate ground.

External claims require evidence appropriate to their claim type: primary sources, authenticated records, measurements, experiments, reliable databases, human observation, or other warranted bridges to reality.

Research becomes inspectable work

Publish the method, the failures, and the counterexamples.

The research workflow turns the existing archive of reasoning chains, experiments, observations, hypotheses, and concepts into authored publications, reproducible demonstrations, new investigations, and engineering requirements.

Failures and counterexamples belong in the record alongside improvements.
