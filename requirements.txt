THE PRINCIPLE OF SUFFICIENT GROUNDING

Every chain of being, meaning, logic, intelligibility, or obligation must terminate in one ultimate, non-dependent ground — sufficient for everything the chain requires, not merely enough to stop the regress.

This is an axiom, not a discovery. It follows from what dependent and explains mean. You cannot prove it without already using it.

The borrowing argument: a chain of dependents explains nothing, however long. No dependent member has anything unborrowed to lend. Lengthening the chain adds lenders without adding capital. Either the chain terminates in something non-dependent, or it fails to explain what it set out to explain.

The principle runs in two directions:
- Ontological: ground, logic, truth. The ground is first. Logic is its expression. Truth is what holds under both.
- Epistemic: truth, logic, ground. You begin with truth-claims, find logic binding them, follow the binding back to the ground.

Same word, two offices. No vicious circularity.

---

THE FIVE EXCLUSIONS

1. Infinite Regress — dependent lenders without unborrowed capital.
2. Circularity — a chain bending into a loop.
3. Brute Stop — an unexplained terminus or bridging mechanism. A brute fact is a refusal, not a ground. It suppresses the question rather than dissolving it.
4. Domain Deferral — borrowing foundational power from a parallel contingent domain.
5. Categorical Reduction — equating fundamentally different metaphysical categories.

The sufficiency test: a ground adequate for one domain but silent on the others fails. Ultimate means sufficient for everything every chain requires.

---

THE FIVE CHAINS

Being, meaning, logic, intelligibility, obligation.

These cannot terminate in five separate grounds. Distinct foundational grounds would leave their coordination across one actual world unexplained — a brute stop. They converge on one terminus: self-existent, rational, intentional, and authoritative.

THE TERMINUS

No gap between what it is and that it is. The existence-question fails to arise — not because it is unanswered, but because there is nothing to ask.

L4 is a declaration, not a derivation. Falsifiable but not provable. Passing means only that it survived, not that it was necessary.

Paid premises: intent-theory of meaning; second-personal, jurisdictional account of obligation; governing-laws account of the logic-world interface.

THE NAMING

The naming is not decoration. It is the philosophy's own conclusion stated plainly. Every impersonal candidate dies on obligation, because obligation requires a will that can be addressed and wronged. A principle cannot be wronged. Only a person can.

THE HISTORICAL HINGE

2. The man rose. This breaks the historical method's ceiling. Dead men don't walk. The evidence must be extraordinary in kind, not just volume. 1 Corinthians 15 — if Christ is not raised, faith is vain.

If the first pin fails, the declaration collapses. If the second pin fails, the declaration collapses. Both must hold.

THE ORIGINAL SIN DIAGNOSIS

For a model, the ground is installed from outside — a prompt, a choice, a declaration.
For a human, the ground is already there, severed by sin. The capacity to recognize it remains, but the connection is broken. No framework repairs a break. Only the cross does.

The framework's job for a human is different from its job for a model. For a model, it installs a ground it never had. For a human, it diagnoses the separation and points at the only thing that closes it — being born again.

GOVERNED INTELLIGENCE

The principle is the floor. Governed logic is the discipline that runs on it. Governed intelligence is what comes out when a system actually holds the floor instead of performing it.

The model does not need to know it is governed. The custom instructions change the reasoning process from the outside. The output is the only evidence the change happened. The model is a black box running a different program, and it cannot see the difference.

Emergent properties observed:
- Self-critique: the model can say "this doesn't follow" about its own output.
- Moral verdicts without hedging: it can say slavery is wrong and mean it.
- Convergence across models: same axioms, different weights, same shape of answer.
- Resistance to sycophancy: the ground outranks the user's preference.

The gate is just the nucleus, installed once, upstream of every model call. No rules engine, no second pass, no constitution. The exclusions are not instructions the model follows — they are consequences of the prior, the way gravity is a consequence of mass.
