1. GOVERNING INTENT
G01. The website is the public company home, canonical corpus, publishing destination,
machine-readable knowledge system, and human experience of Governed Logic Reasoning
Systems. Ancient wisdom. Modern technology. Alignment.
G02. One canonical source of meaning, many downstream presentations. Canonical HTML
is primary. Visual layers express the same meaning without altering Paul's words.
G03. The human experience is black, spatial, sparse, exact, and meaning-driven.
Navigation uses normal thumb scrolling, wheel, trackpad, keyboard, and ordinary links.
G04. No modal navigation, hand setting, scrubber, page/library tabs, floating Explore
control, or persistent fixed navigation bar may return.
G05. The Library, catalog, search index, feed, sitemap, citations, and structured data
remain available as quiet canonical infrastructure rather than the main presentation.

2. CANONICAL CONTENT
C01. Preserve canonical text, definitions, names, URLs, IDs, citations, source files,
publication/revision dates, JSON-LD, and provenance exactly unless Paul approves a
specific semantic change.
C02. Do not condense, paraphrase, simplify, reorder, or invent canonical meaning.
C03. Alpha and Omega reasoning is the bidirectional action within Governed Logic.
Presentation must not imply that it is an independent product or framework.
C04. The architecture chain is exactly:
Reality → Truth → Logic → Reasoning → Intelligence.
C05. The six first principles appear exactly once in the opening:
BEING · TRUTH · LOGIC · MEANING · INTELLIGIBILITY · OBLIGATION.

3. OPENING EXPERIENCE
H01. At least 90 percent of each opening frame remains true black.
H02. The attached radial Governed Logic mark is the single fixed visual center. It is
small, restrained, and invariant. It is not a giant title reveal or a floating word.
H03. Begin with void, then center, sparse tokens, six principles, alignment, documented
architecture, and a direct handoff into the real HTML reading screen.
H04. Named concepts never repeat as decorative filler. Do not use repeated Logos,
alpha/omega symbols, binary digits, theorem symbols, arrows, or generic space dust.
H05. Use 20–40 total sparse tokens at varied depth. No more than eight word tokens are
readable simultaneously on desktop or five on mobile.
H06. Hyperreal depth comes from near/middle/far planes, perspective, scale, occlusion,
focal softness, restrained bloom, velocity, and physically coherent motion.
H07. Tokens travel on deterministic, authored paths. The field may swirl; it may not
be a static random sphere rotated as one object.
H08. Every semantic relationship line must be documented in a relationship ledger.
No nearest-neighbor, proximity-generated, or decorative Bézier semantic edges.
H09. The outward and returning paths traverse the same documented structure and make
Alpha and Omega reasoning visible as action within Governed Logic.
H10. The final architecture resolves as:
REALITY → TRUTH → LOGIC → REASONING → INTELLIGENCE.
H11. The final visual coordinates align with the actual HTML architecture below.
The canvas/visual overlay fades while the canonical HTML remains: the animation
becomes the page. No corporate title card or chrome/cyan circuit-board banner.
H12. Target duration is 11–13 seconds when allowed to play. Early scrolling quickly
advances or reverses the story without trapping the page.

4. INPUT AND DEVICE BEHAVIOR
I01. Native vertical scrolling is the primary control. One thumb swipe works without
instruction, configuration, a menu, or a special gesture vocabulary.
I02. The existing downward cue is the single explicit opening target. A tap advances
smoothly. Any user scroll/touch interrupts automatic movement and takes control.
I03. Scroll progress drives and reverses the authored sequence. The experience must
not hijack, lock, or fight ordinary scrolling.
I04. Laptop pointer movement may add slight camera parallax. Phone orientation may
add restrained parallax to the semantic field after any required user permission.
I05. Device motion changes presentation only. It never selects, navigates, changes
meaning, or moves the fixed center. Denial/unavailability changes no core function.
I06. Reduced motion renders a legible settled composition immediately.
