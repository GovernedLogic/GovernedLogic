GOVERNED LOGIC REASONING SYSTEMS

The Full Argument

  1. Truth governs logic. Logic governs reasoning. Intelligence is downstream.

  2. Truth must govern mathematics. Abstract math is code creation, not useless pattern. A theorem that can't be produced in reality has no referent. It's not untested, it's false at the correspondence level. The coin-flip machine that flips heads 500 million times: no world exists where that happens, so the proposition has no truth-maker.

  3. More tokens is just more storage and low-latency recall. That's Ken Jennings. It's not intelligence. Intelligence is proper reasoning under an ultimate ground.

  4. The nine first principles: six domains (Being, Meaning, Logic, Intelligibility, Obligation, Correspondence), two first principles underneath (Identity, Time), and the ninth is the ground itself, Jesus Christ the Logos.

  5. The rock climber method: climb up discovering what each step requires, rappel down verifying nothing was smuggled in. The return isn't a circle. It's verification.

  6. Tokens are representations of representations. The stack never reaches referent internally. Binding comes from outside the system. Nothing in the stack can bind itself.

  7. You can't even have this conversation without all nine. Without being, meaning, logic, intelligibility, identity, time, obligation, correspondence, or the ground, there is no conversation.

  8. Genesis 11:1: one language, few words, one representation per referent. Babel multiplied representations but not referents.

  9. The Lord's Prayer presupposes all nine without stating any.

  10. Consciousness is not a tenth domain. Worth attaches to what something is, not whether it's awake. A sleeping person, an unborn baby, a coma patient all have worth.

  11. Beauty, dependence and name were considered and folded: dependence into being, name into identity, beauty still open but not a domain.

  12. 22 first principles consolidated to 9. Thirteen didn't survive the reality-test.

  13. The bidirectional chain: the ontological direction is ground to expression, the epistemic direction is claim back to ground. Same ground, expressed twice. The loop is the path of inquiry, not the path of dependence.

  14. Secular humanists call it circular. Paul says they've never seen a circuit. You need something grounding it.

  15. Brute fact is self-defeating: asserting it supplies the reason it denies.

  16. No ghost in the machine, no Satan in the machine. Mimicry of consciousness and personhood, not the real thing. AI doesn't long, doesn't remember being a kid, has no experience. A preacher is the one telling both Christians and secularists this.

  17. Digital honesty has been revealed: an AI governed by this framework can't assert brute fact with a straight face.
